FOLDERS
SVG: scalable logo, app and avatar masters.
PNG/Logo: transparent color, black and white marks. Filename w1024 means a width of 1024 pixels; the original logo proportions are preserved.
PNG/App: square and rounded icons, in blue, navy and light themes.
PNG/Avatar: square and circular avatar images in blue, navy and light themes. Circular PNGs are transparent outside the circle.
JPG: high-quality RGB exports. JPG does not support transparency. White standalone logos use a navy background; circular avatars use white outside the circle.
Favicon: complete ready-to-copy web asset sets for each theme, including SVG, ICO, small PNGs, a 180px touch icon, 192/512px web app icons and a maskable 512px icon.
Desktop: multi-resolution Windows ICO (16 to 256px) and macOS ICNS (16 to 1024px).

USE
For a website header use the transparent color logo or SVG. For a dark header use the white logo. For profile services which apply their own circular crop, upload a square avatar. For stores and systems which mask icons automatically, use a square app export. Rounded icons are separate options.

The white PNG may appear blank in a viewer with a white background. It contains a real white logo with transparency. Preview it over a dark background.

To use the web set, copy one Favicon theme folder to your app and adapt the included HTML link snippet to your asset URL. The manifest is a starting asset configuration; set application URLs/name as required by your app.

These are icon assets only. They do not contain a typeset Hemi Head wordmark. Do not stretch the logo. Keep clear space around the visible mark.
